How a job comes down

From standing to site ready.

  1. Stage 01 / 05

    Set up and strip out

    Fencing up, services checked and the inside stripped first. Steel, timber and brick are separated before the big machine touches a wall.

  2. Stage 02 / 05

    Knock it down

    Our Sumitomo SH125 brings the building down in sections while a hose keeps the dust on the ground and off your neighbours' cars.

  3. Stage 03 / 05

    Load and cart away

    Brick, concrete and soil go straight into tippers and bins. Sorted for recycling wherever we can, docketed every load.

  4. Stage 04 / 05

    Dig it out

    Footings, OSD tanks, basements and pools. Bulk or detailed, with the machine matched to the access.

  5. Stage 05 / 05

    Site ready

    A clean, level block and a builder who can start on day one. That is the job done properly.

Questions

Demolition and excavation questions, answered.

How much does house demolition cost in Sydney?

It depends on the size of the house, what it is built from (double brick, fibro or weatherboard), the access, and whether there is asbestos, a pool, a garage or a slab to come out as well. The fastest way to a real number is to send us the address and a few photos. We will come back with a fixed quote, not a range.

Do you handle the council side of a demolition?

Most demolitions in NSW need an approval first, either a DA or a complying development certificate. We work alongside your builder or certifier, follow the conditions on the approval and provide the paperwork they need from us, including waste dockets.

How narrow can your machines get in?

Our Yanmar ViO17 mini excavator works through access of about one metre, so side passages and backyards are no problem. If your access is tighter than that, ask us about a micro excavator from around 0.7 m. Send a photo of the narrowest point and we will tell you straight.

What happens if there is asbestos on site?

Asbestos is removed by a licensed removalist under NSW rules before the main knock down, with clearance paperwork for your certifier. We have done jobs with asbestos garages and sheds, and we plan the sequence so the site keeps moving.

Do you recycle demolition waste?

Yes. Brick, concrete, steel, timber and green waste are separated on site wherever we can, so more of your old house goes to recycling and less goes to landfill. Every load is docketed.

Can you supply bins and trucks as well?

Yes. We run hook bins for soil, concrete, brick, vegetation and household waste, and tippers for bigger cart away. One crew, one booking, one invoice.
