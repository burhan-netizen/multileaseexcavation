Overview

A knock down without the chaos

Demolition is the first job on your build, and if it goes wrong, everything after it slips. We plan the sequence before we start: services disconnected, fencing up, hazardous materials dealt with, then the building comes down in a controlled order.

Our Sumitomo SH125 does the heavy lifting on full knock downs, with water on the bucket to keep dust down. On tighter sites, our Yanmar minis with hammers and grabs take walls and slabs apart piece by piece. Brick, concrete, steel and timber are separated on site so more of it goes to recycling, and every load is docketed for your certifier.

We work for homeowners, owner builders and builders, from single garages to double brick houses with a pool out the back, and commercial buildings on busy streets.

The process

How it runs, step by step.

  1. Quote and plan

    Site visit, check approval conditions and services, fixed price and start date.

  2. Make safe

    Fencing, signage, services confirmed off, hazardous materials cleared by licensed removalists.

  3. Strip and knock down

    Internals stripped and sorted, then the structure comes down with dust suppression.

  4. Clear and hand over

    Rubble carted, site levelled, dockets handed over for your certifier.

Questions

Demolition FAQs.

How long does a house demolition take?

Most single houses come down and are carted away in a few days to a week once the site is made safe. Double brick, pools, slabs and tight access add time. We give you a start date and a realistic finish date in the quote.

Do I need approval to demolish my house?

Usually yes. In NSW most demolitions need a DA or a complying development certificate. Your builder or certifier normally organises it, and we work to the conditions on that approval.

Who disconnects the services?

Electricity, gas, water and sewer must be disconnected or capped before demolition. We tell you exactly what needs to be done and when, so nothing holds up the start date.

What about asbestos?

Asbestos is removed by a licensed removalist under NSW rules before the main demolition, and a clearance certificate is issued. We have taken down asbestos garages and plan the job around it.

Do you recycle?

Yes. Brick, concrete, steel, timber and green waste are separated on site wherever possible, so less goes to landfill.
